About Me

I'm a 16 year old game developer from Belgium. I started messing around with games when I was 8, and by the time I was 14 I decided to take it seriously. I build in Unreal Engine 5 using the Blueprint system, and I also know basic Python.

I've built 5 games so far, none released yet, but I'm aiming to ship my first one in the next year or two. Every project teaches me something new.

🚗 H2 RC Grand Prix

We were provided with basic materials including HydroSticks and had to build a functional vehicle under strict constraints.

I took initiative across multiple areas due to limited team leadership — coordinating tasks, doing 3D and body design, and handling electronics.

One of our key innovations was a custom shock absorption system to protect the CPU during rear impacts.

I developed practical skills in mechanics, leadership, task planning, and team coordination.

🥚 Egg crash challenge

We had to build a small wooden car entirely from our own imagination.

I surrounded the car with sponges to absorb impact, and designed a cushioned compartment for the egg connected only by rubber bands — so it could move forward and backward and wouldn't stop abruptly.

Bridge

Built a bridge out of small wooden sticks and tape. Target: hold 3–5 kg.

click to learn more →

🌉 Bridge

Built in a group of 3 with only 4 hours of work time per week over 2 weeks.

We researched different bridge designs, then went with an interlocked stick design reinforced with tape.

On test day our bridge was dropped on the way to the test — it still held 3.5 kg and we scored around 60%.

click to learn more →

🕷️ Spider robot

Wire feelers acted as whiskers — when one bent on contact with an object, the spider turned in the opposite direction.

Components: a battery, a small vibration motor, wires, and button-style switches with long handles for the whiskers.

My first real hands-on experience with basic electronics.
